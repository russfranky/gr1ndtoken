# Encrypted Biometrics for Proof-of-Personhood: 2026 State of the Art
*Research date: 2026-10-03. Confidence flags: `verified live` = read from a live primary page this run; `index` = from search-index content; secondary = GitHub research docs / press. Where evidence was thin or conflicting it is flagged inline.*

## Summary
- **The only biometric proof-of-personhood system at production scale with on-chain verification in Oct 2026 is World ID (World, formerly Worldcoin): ~18M Orb-verified humans, 160+ countries, growth plateaued in 2026.** Its "encrypted biometrics" = iris codes computed on-device, secret-shared to independent MPC custodians (AMPC/SMPC), uniqueness via encrypted-domain comparison revealing only match/no-match, and anonymous set-membership ZK proofs (Semaphore/Groth16) with per-context nullifiers. World ID 4.0 (launched Apr 17, 2026) moved to account abstraction, one-time-use nullifiers via OPRF nodes, and tiered credentials (Orb > NFC document > Selfie).
- **Humanity Protocol is effectively disqualified for now**: June 2026 private-key hack (~$32–36M, DPRK-attributed), token −80%, mainnet relaunch pending, pivot away from proof-of-personhood toward "Proof of Trust"/enterprise AI; its headline "8M Human IDs" were referral reservations, not palm verifications.
- **Human Passport (ex-Gitcoin Passport) is live on Base via EAS attestations but is a behavioral aggregate score, not a biometric uniqueness oracle** — purchasable stamps mean it prices Sybil resistance at ~tens of dollars, not prevents it.
- **Holonym's Human Keys (threshold OPRF over biometrics + homomorphic computation on encrypted data, EigenLayer AVS)** is the closest thing to "encrypted biometrics" as a key-derivation primitive, but its anonymous-biometric product is still pilot-stage.
- **UIID (Linkspreed) is a new 2026 identity-wallet entrant with vendor-only evidence** — unverifiable claims, no adoption numbers, and its uniqueness root is document/KYC-based, not biometric dedup.
- **Regulatory reality: World is banned/restricted in Spain, Portugal, Hong Kong, Kenya (court-ordered data deletion, completed), Indonesia, Brazil, South Korea (ban + ~$800k fine), Thailand, Philippines, plus investigations in Germany/France/India/Colombia — while expanding in the US.** Net effect: flat Orb-verification growth in 2026 and a strategic pivot to lower-assurance credentials (Selfie/NFC) and enterprise deals.
- **Recommendation (see §6): gate registration with cheap layers (device attestation + Human Passport signal + behavioral), and put the World ID Orb biometric gate only at high-value claim points (top rarity tiers, high-frequency claimants). Verify off-chain via World ID 4.0 + relay attestations to the Base contract, because v4 on-chain verification is World-Chain-only; keep the legacy v3 on-chain path on Base as fallback. Residual risk is dominated by purchased/farmed verified humans, not by cryptography.**

## 1. Landscape: anonymity-preserving biometric identity systems (2026)

### 1.1 World / World ID (formerly Worldcoin) — LIVE, largest by far
- **Scale:** ~18M Orb-verified unique humans as of Mar 2026 (CoinDesk), still ~18M in Jul 2026 — **Orb-verification growth visibly plateaued in 2026**; ~38–40M World App accounts; ~475M cumulative World ID proofs issued (CoinDesk, 2026-07-24); 160+ countries; ~1,300+ Orbs. Historical arc: 2M (mid-2023) → 6M (Sep 2024) → 12M (May 2025) → 18M (Mar 2026); the 50M end-2025 target was missed. WLD trades ~$0.27–0.53, ~95–97% below its ~$11.74 ATH (Mar 2024). (`index`: https://crypto.news/pi-network-vs-worldcoin-the-proof-of-human-war-nobody-is-winning-yet/ ; https://www.ainvest.com/news/worldcoin-wld-faces-tokenomics-pressure-biometric-identity-adoption-2609/ ; https://github.com/andrevalenm/poh-aggregator/blob/HEAD/research/protocols/world-id.md)
- **Iris pipeline (cryptographic, primary sources):** The Orb captures multispectral iris/face imagery and computes an **IrisCode** on-device (a numeric code, not an image); source images are deleted by default, with an opt-in for temporary retention for algorithm training. The iris photo is sent as an end-to-end encrypted bundle to the user's phone. Uniqueness checking runs under **AMPC (anonymized multi-party computation)**: iris codes and iris masks are secret-shared and end-to-end encrypted to separate compute nodes (as of Jan 2025: Nethermind, FAU Erlangen-Nuremberg, UC Berkeley RDI; KAIST also a node), which jointly compute similarity and reveal only a binary match/no-match — no plaintext Hamming distances, no plaintext masks at any stage. The SMPC system is open-source (audited by Least Authority); the legacy iris-code database was deleted May 2024 and the SMPC fragments Jan 2025; "full codes are only stored on the user's devices." (`verified live`: https://world.org/blog/announcements/worldcoin-foundation-unveils-new-smpc-system-deletes-iris-codes ; `index`: https://whitepaper.world.org/)
- **Why iris (primary):** irises carry more random unique detail than fingerprints or faces, are stable over time, and are harder to steal/fake than fingerprints; commercial iris hardware was deemed insufficient, hence the custom Orb. (`verified live`: https://support.world.org/hc/en-us/articles/36864436571795-Understanding-biometrics-and-their-use-by-World)
- **World ID 4.0 (launched Apr 17, 2026, "Lift Off" SF):** account-based architecture (WorldIDRegistry), multi-key, key rotation, recovery agent, multi-device sessions, **one-time-use nullifiers generated via OPRF nodes** (each verification yields a fresh unlinkable token per relying party), a Relying Party Registry (explicitly "laying the groundwork for future protocol fees"), an open-source SDK, and a separate World ID app. Three credential tiers: **Orb** (highest assurance), **Official ID/NFC** (~13 countries, high), **Selfie Check** (medium, low friction; "not as robust as the Orb"). Nullifier scoping changed from per-`app_id`+`action` (v3) to per-`rp_id` one-time-use (v4), closing a cross-app correlation hole. Enterprise traction: Zoom (Deep Face 3-way match: signed Orb reference image + live on-device face scan + live video frame), DocuSign, Tinder, Okta Human Principal (beta), Visa card, Razer gaming, Shopify. (`index`: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260417530721/en/The-New-World-ID-Proof-of-Human-for-the-AI-Era-Scales-Across-the-Digital-Platforms-People-and-Businesses-Use-Every-Day ; https://mobileidworld.com/world-ships-world-id-4-0-app-with-zoom-docusign-integrations/ ; https://www.computerworld.com/article/4160511/world-id-expands-its-proof-of-human-vision-for-the-ai-era.html ; https://medium.com/@godbrand0/world-id-4-0-what-changed-and-why-it-matters-2dedc1c41f8d)
- **AgentKit (Mar 2026):** verified humans can delegate to AI agents, which carry a ZK proof of human backing — described as "a power of attorney for your agent." Relevant to the game: agent-delegated proofs are a designed feature, so the game must bind proofs to specific actions/nonces. (`index`: https://github.com/piiiico/proof-of-commitment/blob/HEAD/knowledge/proof-of-personhood.md ; https://www.computerworld.com/article/4160511/world-id-expands-its-proof-of-human-vision-for-the-ai-era.html)
- **Trust model:** trust the Orb hardware + operator network for capture integrity; trust the AMPC custodian set (independent universities/firms) for non-collusion on secret shares; trust Tools for Humanity/World Foundation for the WorldIDRegistry and the upgradeable verifier proxy (World can swap verification logic — pin and monitor the implementation slot if verifying on-chain). The ZK proof hides identity but **the uniqueness property is an assertion by the issuer wrapped in a ZK proof** — it is not trustless.

### 1.3 Human Passport (formerly Gitcoin Passport) — LIVE, but a signal, not an oracle
- Acquired by Holonym Foundation (Dec 2024, $10M); part of human.tech (Passport, Network, Wallet). 2M+ users, 35M+ credentials being migrated to ZK-friendly credentials; defended 9 Gitcoin rounds; 120+ projects, $512M+ capital flow secured (per its own Mar 2026 blog). (`index`: https://human.tech/blog/human-passport-proof-of-personhood-and-sybil-resistance-for-web3 ; https://Cointelegraph.Com/news/zk-identity-holonym-acquire-gitcoin-passport-10-million)
- Stamp aggregator → 0–100 Unique Humanity Score, readable via REST or **on-chain EAS attestations on Optimism, Base, Arbitrum, Linea, Scroll, Shape, zkSync Era**; scores expire after ~90 days. (`index`: https://github.com/andrevalenm/poh-aggregator/blob/HEAD/research/protocols/passport-civic-fractal-zkme-galxe.md)
- **Trust model / weakness:** mostly behavioral/account-ownership stamps (Google, X, GitHub, wallet age) with a minority of high-weight uniqueness stamps (Holonym gov-ID/biometrics, Binance BABT, Coinbase). Independent analysis prices a passing score at ~$20 via purchasable wallet activity (or ~$1 for a rented aged wallet) — it is Sybil-*resistant*, not Sybil-*proof*, and the weights are vendor policy changeable off-chain. **Use as a cheap supplementary gate on Base, never as the uniqueness oracle.** (flag: independent-research figures, `index`-only)

### 1.4 Holonym / Human Keys — the "encrypted biometrics" key primitive (pilot-stage product)
- **Mishti Network**: a threshold entropy network computing an **Oblivious Pseudorandom Function (OPRF) over biometric data**, letting humans derive random, hard-to-steal keys from biometrics; it "unlocks private homomorphic computation on encrypted data, allowing untrusted third-parties to authenticate users without ever seeing the underlying data." An EigenLayer AVS (Ethereum crypto-economic security). Zeronym (ZK identity): 125k+ pseudonymous users (2024). An anonymous-biometric method was being piloted for displaced populations; Human Passport was slated to add it. (`index`: https://cryptobriefing.com/holonym-foundation-emerges-with-5-5-million-seed-funding-to-provide-global-digital-personhood-with-human-keys/)
- This is the closest deployed analog to textbook "encrypted biometrics" (biometric → key via OPRF; matching over encrypted data), but the anonymous-biometric credential is not a demonstrated at-scale uniqueness oracle in 2026. Watch, don't build on.

### 1.5 UIID (Linkspreed LLC) — new 2026 entrant, vendor evidence only
- **What it is:** "Universal Integrated Identity Decoupled," the identity layer of Linkspreed's "Web4" stack. Two-tier design: a never-disclosed Core Identifier + context-scoped Alias Identifiers (Verifiable Credentials). Local-first: Ed25519 logins resolved on-device, biometric enrollment material stays on device, claimed Groth16 ZKPs for attribute proofs (age etc.), WebAuthn passwordless, eIDAS 2.0 NFC-passport and NIST 800-63B compliance claims, OIDC SDKs. A May 2026 PR claims "Proof of Personhood: advanced biometric anchoring"; Play Store launch was announced May 10, 2026; company blog reports v1.7 shipped and v2.10.2 released. A late-Sep 2026 YouTube deep-dive frames it as "proof of personhood without surveillance." (`verified live`: https://uiid.me ; https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXya3yeVnv8 ; `index`: https://blog.linkspreed.com/blog/web4-sovereign-architecture/ ; https://web4.medium.com/linkspreed-llc-announces-impending-google-play-store-launch-for-flagship-uiid-and-vivon-983d1005f0df)
- **Assessment:** all evidence is vendor-published; no adoption numbers, no independent audit, no demonstrated cross-device biometric dedup. Its own site roots the Core Identity in a one-time KYC — i.e., **document-based uniqueness, not biometric uniqueness**. Treat as an identity wallet with good marketing, not a PoP oracle.

### 1.6 Apple/Google on-device biometrics — do NOT solve Sybil
- Face ID/Touch ID and Android biometrics unlock device-held keys (Secure Enclave/StrongBox). They prove *this device's enrolled user is present*, binding one **device** to one **finger/face template**. They say nothing about whether that human already enrolled on another device, or whether five phones belong to one farm operator. Device-bound ≠ person-bound. This is why the mission's framing is correct: useful for session continuity and per-action re-auth, useless as the uniqueness root.

### 1.7 Other notable 2026 names
- **Privado ID** (ex-Polygon ID), **Idena** (AI-resistant ceremonies), **BrightID** (social graph), **Civic**, **Proof of Humanity** (Kleros-curated registry), **Self.xyz** (ZK passport, Celo), **Zeronym** (Holonym), **Billions.Network** — none combines biometric uniqueness + production on-chain verification at World ID's scale; not deeply researched here.

## 2. Cryptography: how "unique human, anonymous" is actually achieved

### 2.1 The deployed recipe (World ID) — primitives named
1. **Biometric capture → template on trusted hardware.** Orb computes IrisCode on-device from multispectral imagery; raw images deleted (default).
2. **Uniqueness check under MPC, not in the clear.** Iris code + iris masks are **secret-shared** (Shamir-style sharing, exact threshold not published) and end-to-end encrypted to independent AMPC custodian nodes; the MPC returns only **match/no-match**. This is *secure multi-party computation*, not homomorphic encryption. Prior to 2024 the comparison used plaintext pairwise Hamming distances — the upgrade's whole point was to stop any single party seeing codes. (`index`: https://world.org/blog/announcements/worldcoin-foundation-unveils-new-smpc-system-deletes-iris-codes ; https://whitepaper.world.org/)
3. **Anonymous set membership via Semaphore-variant ZK (Groth16).** After passing uniqueness, the user's World ID public key joins a Merkle set rooted on-chain (Ethereum canonical; bridged to Base/Optimism/Polygon/World Chain). To verify, the user proves in ZK: "I know a secret key whose public key is in the set with root R." Nothing else leaks.
4. **Nullifier scheme (the double-signaling brake).** The circuit computes `nullifierHash = H(secretKey, externalNullifier)` where `externalNullifier` binds the relying party's `app_id` + `action` (v3). The same human always yields the same nullifier **within one context** and an unlinkable one across contexts. The verifier (your contract) stores used nullifiers per action — one human, one claim per action. v4 upgrades this to **one-time-use nullifiers from OPRF nodes**, scoped to the relying party (`rp_id`), so even colluding apps cannot correlate a user. (`index`: https://github.com/worldcoin/developer-docs/blob/HEAD/world-id/reference/contracts.mdx ; https://medium.com/@godbrand0/world-id-4-0-what-changed-and-why-it-matters-2dedc1c41f8d)
5. **Signal binding.** An optional user message (e.g., the game's wallet address S) is committed inside the proof (`signalHash`), preventing proof replay against a different wallet.

### 2.2 The "encrypted biometrics" phrase, disambiguated
In shipping systems the term covers three different things — do not conflate them:
- **(a) Encrypted/secret-shared templates for the uniqueness check** (World AMPC/SMPC): templates exist only as secret shares across mutually distrusting custodians; full codes live only on user devices.
- **(b) Biometric-derived keys via threshold OPRF** (Holonym Mishti): the biometric never leaves the device in usable form; a threshold network obliviously evaluates a PRF on it, yielding a stable key; authentication/derivation happens over encrypted data (homomorphic computation). This is the primitive closest to "biometrics you can't steal because there's nothing to steal."
- **(c) On-device matching with platform biometrics** (Apple/Google, UIID-style wallets): the biometric unlocks a local key; the network sees only signatures. No cross-device uniqueness.
- **What is NOT deployed:** general fully-homomorphic-encryption template comparison at scale. The closest is academic: a Sep 2026 *Frontiers in Physics* paper (Olushola & Meenakshi, DOI 10.3389/fphy.2026.1849257) fusing face+iris+fingerprint under ML-KEM-1024 (Kyber) + CKKS FHE at 65–75 ms/auth, 99.99% accuracy — **research-stage only**, no production deployment. (`verified live`: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physics/articles/10.3389/fphy.2026.1849257/full)

### 2.3 What the on-chain verifier actually checks
- **World ID 3.0 (live on Base today):** call `WorldIdRouter.verifyProof(root, groupId=1, signalHash, nullifierHash, externalNullifierHash, proof[8])`. The router routes to the bridged World ID contract (root arrives via the Ethereum→Base state bridge, ~5 min lag); the contract verifies the Groth16 proof against the root and reverts on failure. Your contract must additionally enforce `nullifiers[nullifierHash]` uniqueness — the router does not dedupe for you. Only Orb credentials verify on-chain in v3. (`index`: https://github.com/worldcoin/developer-docs/blob/HEAD/world-id/reference/contracts.mdx)
- **World ID 4.0:** a `WorldIDVerifier` contract exposing `verify(nullifier, action, rpId, nonce, signalHash, expiresAtMin, issuerSchemaId, credentialGenesisIssuedAtMin, proof[5])` — **deployed on World Chain only** (third-party research; proxy reported at `0x00000000009E00F9FE82CfeeBB4556686da094d7`, unconfirmed by me). The `issuerSchemaId` argument means one verifier handles Orb/NFC/Selfie credentials — **a contract must check which schema was proven or it will silently accept the weaker Selfie credential.** Proofs are bound to the relying party (`rpId`) and carry expiry/freshness fields. Off-chain, the supported path is the Developer Portal `/api/v4/verify/{rp_id}` API with an RP-signed request context. (flag: v4 on-chain details from `index`-only secondary research: https://github.com/andrevalenm/poh-aggregator/blob/HEAD/research/protocols/world-id.md)

### 2.4 Known breaks and weaknesses (honest list)
- **Enrollment fraud, not crypto breaks.** Black markets for verified World IDs exist (China, 2023: paid scans delivered to third parties; ~$20 fake scans); Forbes-reported exploits let single individuals create multiple accounts (World: "a few hundred instances"). The iris dedup is only as strong as Orb-operator integrity and fraud detection. (`index`: https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2023/05/24/black-market-for-worldcoin-credentials-pops-up-in-china?ref=blockhead.co ; https://kr-asia.com/identity-in-the-ai-era-an-assessment-of-worldcoins-iris-based-biometric-solution)
- **One person, one enrollment — but the credential is transferable.** Iris dedup blocks double-enrollment, but nothing stops a verified human from selling/renting their World App + nullifier. Mitigation must be at the binding layer (bind nullifier→wallet S at first claim; require fresh proofs for high-value actions).
- **Template irreversibility is a claim, not a theorem.** Iris codes are designed as non-invertible, and World deletes images by default — but "cannot be reverse-engineered" is a vendor assertion; the EFF's standing warning applies: biometric data is "largely unchangeable and difficult to obscure… You only have one body" (Rory Mir, via https://cointelegraph.com/magazine/worldcoin-ban-privacy-regulation-ai). Breach consequence is permanent: the 2026 record shows why — MSG's 26M-record leak included facial-recognition surveillance data; an Aug 2026 unsecured bucket exposed ~9M facial images; a 2026 industry report tallied 88 ID-verification incidents with 2.15B confirmed records, 41 leaking actual templates/ID scans/selfies. (`index`: https://www.pkware.com/blog/2026-data-breaches?ref=hackernoon.com ; https://www.cns-tec.com/blog/9-million-facial-recognition-images-exposed-in-major-breach/ ; https://securityaffairs.com/197855/reports/88-id-verification-breaches-show-the-cost-of-collecting-identity-data.html)
- **Presentation attacks** at capture (the Orb is custom hardened hardware, but no public red-team results were found) and, more practically, **social/operator fraud**.
- **Trust assumptions that remain:** Orb hardware/firmware integrity; AMPC custodians not colluding; World Foundation not abusing the upgradeable verifier/registry; the state bridge delivering correct roots. None is verifiable by the game.

### 2.5 The academic ideal: "personhood credentials" (Adler et al., 2024)
For conceptual completeness: 32 researchers (OpenAI, Microsoft, MIT, Harvard; arXiv:2408.07892; SpruceID participating) proposed **personhood credentials (PHCs)** — offline in-person issuance (passport or biometric for one-per-person dedup, identity *not* linked to the credential), credential stored on-device, online presentation via **zero-knowledge proof** with unlinkability, optional per service, issuer choice, periodic re-auth. **Status: non-peer-reviewed proposal, no deployment found.** It is the cleanest statement of the target, and World ID 4.0 is its closest deployed approximation. (`index`: https://blog.spruceid.com/spruceid-joins-harvard-and-microsoft-researchers-for-new-personhood-credential-proposal/ ; https://www.technologyreview.com/2024/09/02/1103466/how-personhood-credentials-could-help-prove-youre-a-human-online/)

## 3. Regulatory and adoption reality 2026
- **Banned/restricted:** Spain (AEPD ban Mar 18, 2024; appeal rejected), Portugal (90-day ban), Hong Kong (May 22, 2024 — 10-year retention for AI training ruled unjustified; operations halted), Kenya (suspension Aug 2023 → High Court May 5, 2025: unlawful collection, no proper consent, no DPIA → court-ordered deletion within 7 days under ODPC supervision, confirmed completed), Indonesia (suspended May 4, 2025), South Korea (operational ban + ~$800k fine), Brazil (ANPD barred local servicing), Thailand (deletion ordered Nov 2025), Philippines (Oct 2025, "exploitation of vulnerable populations"); investigations/restrictions also in Germany (BayLDA, the EU one-stop-shop lead authority), France, India, Colombia, Argentina. (`index`: https://cointelegraph.com/magazine/worldcoin-ban-privacy-regulation-ai ; https://blockonomi.com/worldcoin-deletes-kenyan-biometric-data-following-court-order/ ; https://techpoint.africa/news/worldcoin-deletes-kenyans-data/ ; https://www.ainvest.com/news/worldcoin-suspended-indonesia-privacy-concerns-2505/)

## 4. Game integration: on-chain game on Base

### 4.1 Cost
- **Gas:** verifying a Groth16 Semaphore proof on-chain (v3 `verifyProof` via the Base Router) is an L2 transaction costing a few hundred thousand gas units — at Base gas prices this is **cents to sub-cent per verification**, but I could not verify an exact figure from any published source; measure on Base Sepolia at integration time. The proof itself is `view` (eth_call-able), so a *check* is free; only state-changing calls (recording the nullifier) cost gas.
- **Protocol fees:** **no per-verification protocol fee schedule was found.** World announced (Apr 2026) that "apps will pay a fee, not humans" when requesting World ID proofs, enabled by the v4 Relying Party Registry — but no price list is published. **Budget for a future per-proof app fee; confirm with World before committing.** (flag: `index`: https://www.computerworld.com/article/4160511/world-id-expands-its-proof-of-human-vision-for-the-ai-era.html)
- **Enrollment cost to the user:** free (World pays Orb operators; users historically received WLD grants). The binding constraint is physical Orb access, not money.

### 4.2 User flow (recommended: v4 off-chain verify + on-chain attestation relay)
1. Player verifies once at an Orb → World ID in the World ID app (or World App).
2. At the gated game action (e.g., claiming a top-tier item), the game client opens the IDKit widget with an **RP-signed request context** (`rp_id`, nonce, action, expiry).
3. Player approves in the World ID app; the wallet generates the ZK proof locally.
4. Game backend forwards the response to World's **`/api/v4/verify/{rp_id}`** (or verifies the v4 proof against the World Chain `WorldIDVerifier`); on success it checks the returned `verification_level`/credential schema = Orb.
5. Backend (or a permissionless relayer) submits the attestation to the game's Base contract, which records `nullifier → game key S` and enforces one-claim-per-nullifier-per-action. **The signal should be the player's S address**, binding the proof to that wallet and killing replay to other wallets.
6. Fallback path: v3 legacy Orb proofs verified **directly on Base** via the WorldIdRouter (`groupId=1`); contract stores `nullifierHash`. Works today, but it is the legacy path and `allow_legacy_proofs`-style migration handling is needed.

### 4.3 Unlinkability: can verification avoid linking wallet S to the biometric identity?
- **Yes, at the biometric layer:** the game never sees an iris code, image, or identity. It sees only a nullifier (a per-relying-party pseudonym) and a validity bit. Under v4's one-time-use, rp-scoped nullifiers, even World cannot correlate the player's verifications across different apps (multi-party entropy).
- **Within the game, linkage is intentional:** the contract must map nullifier→S to prevent double claims. That mapping is a stable pseudonym *inside the game only*. Design the contract so the mapping cannot be re-pointed to a new S (prevents account-sale re-binding), but note a sold World ID + game account transfers as a bundle — residual risk.
- **Caveat:** v3 nullifiers are stable per (app, action); two apps by the same operator could correlate. Use v4 flows to avoid this.

### 4.4 Failure modes
- **User loses access:** v3 was device-bound (lose device/key = lose World ID). v4 adds key rotation, multi-authenticator, and a Recovery Agent — materially better, but recovery still depends on World's account infrastructure. Design the game to allow the player to re-prove and re-bind after recovery (with a cooldown + old-binding invalidation).
- **Re-verification needs:** Human Passport scores expire at 90 days; World v4 proofs carry `expiresAtMin` and credential-age fields — require fresh proofs for high-value claims rather than one-time lifetime verification.
- **One human, multiple Orbs:** iris dedup is the defense; residual risk is operator fraud and black-market enrollment (documented at small scale).
- **Coverage:** players in banned jurisdictions or far from Orbs (~1,300+) cannot enroll — the gate must degrade gracefully (queue/allowlist, not silent exclusion), or the game loses those markets.
- **Trust/upgrade risk:** the v4 verifier is an upgradeable proxy controlled by World; the state bridge is trusted for roots. Pin, monitor, and keep the game's gate behind an upgradeable (UUPS) contract so verification logic can migrate.
- **Protocol-fee risk:** future per-proof app fees could change the economics of per-claim verification; prefer per-account binding (verify once per S, reuse binding) over per-claim proofs.

## 5. Tradeoffs vs alternatives
| Layer | What it proves | Cost/friction | Defeats | Fails against |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **World ID Orb (biometric PoP)** | A unique living human enrolled | High friction (in-person Orb); ~18M coverage | Account farms, multi-accounting per human | Purchased/farmed verified humans; banned jurisdictions; operator fraud |
| **World ID Selfie/NFC** | Liveness / document-backed personhood | Low-medium friction | Casual bots, duplicate accounts | Weaker uniqueness; spoofable (selfie) |
| **Device attestation (App Attest / Play Integrity)** | Genuine untampered device + genuine app binary | Near-zero friction, free at low volume | Emulators, rooted devices, repackaged clients | **Bot farms buy real phones** (device farms); proves device, never person |
| **Human Passport score** | Aggregate web2/web3 reputation | Low friction | Throwaway wallets | Purchasable stamps (~$20 forge / ~$1 rent per independent analysis — thin evidence) |
| **Stake/slash deposits** | Capital at risk per account | Prices out legit players; needs adjudication | Low-budget Sybil | Capital-rich attackers; punishes honest users more (per SWARM research note) |
| **Social-graph vouching** | Web-of-trust membership | Slow, low coverage | Cheap bots | Vouch markets, collusion, poor UX |

- **Device attestation specifics:** Apple App Attest = Secure-Enclave-attested per-app key; backend validates the attestation object once against Apple's roots, then verifies per-request assertions — iOS-only, and it attests the *key*, not a clean runtime (no jailbreak/hook detection). Google Play Integrity = verdict labels (`MEETS_DEVICE_INTEGRITY` = hardware-backed verified boot on Android 13+, `MEETS_STRONG_INTEGRITY` opt-in, etc.), requires Play Services, coarse labels, quotas/latency. Both are *device* roots of trust. (`index`: https://medium.com/@talsec/eudi-app-attestation-choices-appicrypt-vs-google-play-integrity-apple-app-attest-206d26fe349d ; https://github.com/reality2byte/maswe/blob/HEAD/weaknesses/MASVS-RESILIENCE/MASWE-0056.md)
- **Field consensus (confirmed): tiered, layered defense.** Cheap broad gates for everyone (attestation + behavioral + Passport-grade signals), expensive high-assurance biometric gates only where value concentrates. World ID 4.0 itself is tiered (Selfie < NFC < Orb). The tdcommons defensive publication (Sep 2026) is a useful adjacent pattern — per-action FIDO2/WebAuthn on an attested authenticator + proximity — but note it is *device*-bound personhood, not a uniqueness oracle; it also does not mention Worldcoin (the lead's claim about it could not be verified). (`verified live`: https://www.tdcommons.org/dpubs_series/11867)

## 6. Recommendation for the game design

**Place the biometric gate at the claim, not at registration — and only for high-value tiers plus high-frequency claimants.**
- **Registration / low tiers:** Play Integrity + App Attest (genuine device/app), Human Passport score threshold (read on-chain EAS attestation on Base, 90-day expiry), ticket/rate limiting, behavioral anomaly detection. This keeps onboarding global and free.
- **Biometric gate triggers:** (a) claiming any item above a rarity/value threshold the designers set; (b) any account whose claim frequency exceeds N claims per epoch (the "high-frequency claimant" tripwire — this is exactly where ticket-farming bot rings show up); (c) withdrawals/bridging of rare items off the game.
- **Which system: World ID, Orb credential only.** It is the sole biometric PoP with production scale, on-chain verification, and a real nullifier scheme. Reject Humanity Protocol (down/pivoting), UIID (unproven vendor claims), and Human Passport as the uniqueness root (keep it as a cheap signal).

**Exact verification steps (Base):**
1. Deploy the gate contract behind a UUPS proxy. Store `mapping(bytes32 nullifier => address boundS)` and `mapping(bytes32 nullifier => bool claimed[rarityTier])`.
2. **Primary path (v4):** game backend creates an RP-signed request context (`rp_id`, action = e.g. `"claim-legendary"`, nonce, short expiry); IDKit widget → World ID app → proof generated on-device; backend verifies via `/api/v4/verify/{rp_id}`, asserts credential schema = Orb (PoH) and `verification_level` = orb; then a relayer submits `(S, nullifier, action)` to the Base contract, which binds nullifier→S on first use (immutable thereafter) and marks the tier claimed. Set the **signal = S** so the proof is wallet-bound.
3. **Fallback path (v3, fully on-chain on Base):** accept legacy Orb proofs through the Base `WorldIdRouter.verifyProof` with `groupId=1`, same nullifier-store pattern. Useful if the v4 backend path is down; plan to deprecate.
4. **Freshness:** require a fresh proof (short `expiresAt`) for each high-tier claim; do not accept a years-old binding for new high-value claims — this blunts the "bought verified account" attack.
5. **Agent-delegation hygiene:** because World ID 4.0 AgentKit lets humans delegate to agents, require the nonce to be game-issued per claim and the signal to name the claiming wallet, so a delegated agent cannot replay one proof across many claims.

**Honest residual risks (what a determined attacker still does):**
- **Buys verified humans.** The dominant attack is economic, not cryptographic: pay people ~$20–55 to scan (documented black markets), then operate their accounts. Iris dedup cannot see this. Mitigations: per-claim fresh proofs, claim-velocity tripwires, binding immutability, and making the *game account* valuable enough that selling it hurts (progression, reputation).
- **Coverage exclusion.** ~18M verified humans is ~0.2% of humanity, skewed to permissive jurisdictions; a hard Orb gate forfeits banned regions (Spain, Brazil, Kenya, Indonesia, etc.). Keep a non-biometric path with lower limits.
- **Trust in World.** Upgradeable verifier proxy, state bridge, Orb-operator integrity, and a future per-proof app fee schedule are all trusted/external. Monitor the proxy implementation slot; budget fee risk.
- **No unlinkability *inside* the game.** The nullifier→S map is a deliberate pseudonymous link; protect it as sensitive (it reveals which game wallets belong to distinct humans).
- **What biometrics can never fix:** a bot farm that *actually plays* with one verified human per N accounts is indistinguishable from N casual players — the biometric gate caps accounts-per-human at one, which converts the attacker's problem from "infinite free accounts" to "recruit one human per account," i.e., it raises marginal cost from ~$0 to ~tens of dollars per Sybil. Size tier rewards and deposit/stake parameters against that price, not against zero.
