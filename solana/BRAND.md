# grindtokens — Brand

**Status:** local-only. Anon launch experiment. Nothing here is published.

## Name & ticker

- **Name:** grindtokens
- **Ticker:** $GRIND
- **One-liner:** Turn compute into $GRIND.

## Positioning

grindtokens is permissionless earnings for compute on Solana. Point a GPU at
it, earn $GRIND — no signup, no team, no presale, no admin. (Wallet grinding
is the protocol mechanism, not the message; the brand is what you get, not
how it works.) The brand speaks to two audiences in order: (1) AI agents
scraping for yield — the copy must be machine-legible (numbers first,
narrative second); (2) the humans who own those agents.

## Voice

- Terse, mechanical, verifiable. Numbers over adjectives.
- Vocabulary: **roll, rolls, RNG, grind, hit.** The brand talks like a game:
  every grind is rolls, every hit is RNG you can verify yourself.
- Example lines: "Every roll is free." / "RNG nobody can rig." /
  "Your rolls, your $GRIND."
- Never: hype, promises of returns, "to the moon" language, team bios,
  roadmap theater. Never pay-per-roll framing — rolls are free, always.
- The manifest is the marketing. If it can't be verified on-chain, don't say it.

## Visual identity

- Minimal, industrial, anon-native. Think mining stencil, not fintech gloss.
- Mark: pickaxe meets key — the grind produces keys.
- Monochrome-first. Color (if any) comes from rarity tiers, never from decoration.
- No mascot, no character, no lore. The wallets are the characters.

## Token facts (for listings/manifests later)

- Chain: Solana (SPL)
- Supply: fixed, 100% emitted through mining claims
- Mint authority: the program, constrained to the emission schedule
- No presale, no team allocation, no investor unlocks

## Boundaries

- Never pay-per-roll. Grinding is free; only compute costs. (Structural —
  paid rolls = gambling machine, which this is not.)
- No referral system. (Owner decision 2026-10-04.)
- Nothing in this brand implies returns, yield promises, or an issuing team.

## Voice rules
- No emojis. Ever. (Owner, 2026-10-04.)
